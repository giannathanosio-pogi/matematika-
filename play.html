About Matematika

Matematika turns Patintero, the classic Filipino tag-line game, into a math review. Cross every line to the far end, then run back to the start while the taggers patrol the lines. When one touches you, a question pops up. Answer correctly and quickly to slip through. Answer wrong, or too slowly, and you lose a life.

📚12 topicsEvery chapter of the Mathematics in the Modern World reviewer
🎯4 levelsEasy, Moderate, Intermediate and Hard, with faster taggers each time
Power-upsGrab lives, shields and extra answer time on the court
📊Your resultsAccuracy and speed per level, plus tips on what to study next

Use Review Topics to read the chapters, the formula sheet and the practice questions before you play. Use the gear button to change the music and sound effects.
